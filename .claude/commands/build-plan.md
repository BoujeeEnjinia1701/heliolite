Record every change in a new decision record docs/decisions/NNNN-design-for-construction.md (DDR) with the reason. Anything that would change what the product does, its pitch or its safety case is proposed, not made. Regenerate STEP and STL, the general arrangement drawing (bump its revision) and the concept media from the updated model. Set `design_state: constructable` in project.yaml.

Add a dated section to docs/REVIEW.md listing every design change made for construction. Run `python .kit/render.py --check`, commit, push, then stop.
